FAQ

Questions Parents Ask

What percentage of the day is taught in each language?
50/50 from Elementary up: subjects are split by language (Math & Science in English, French & Humanities in French, rotated by term), so both languages carry the full curriculum — not just one subject each.
Do parents need to speak the second language at home?
No. Around 30% of our families are not fluent in the second language at home — the school carries the immersion. We send a weekly 'practice corner' (5 minutes a day) to help at home.
What are the application deadlines?
Rolling, with priority rounds on 31 January and 30 April for the following academic year. Early Years keeps a waiting list every term — the sooner you visit, the sooner you apply.
Is transportation provided?
Yes — six bus routes covering Limbe, Buea and Mundemba, with GPS tracking shared to parents every morning and afternoon.
