Why Saint Anne's

A Standard That Is Lived

The Dean's Desk

After the first sequence, the cream of the class — 15+ averages, all papers passed — earn a special outing with the academic deans. Merit is a practice here, not a slogan.

Two Clear Streams

Grammar (Arts & Sciences) and Commercial (Marketing, Accounting) — a pathway chosen early and taught deeply.

Faith & Formation

A Catholic school in the Diocese of Buea — discipline, service and character formed alongside the curriculum.

A Full School Week

Classes run Monday to Saturday — 7:30 to 15:30 on week days, a half day on Saturday. A week that is taught, not rushed.

FAQ

Questions Parents Ask

What streams does the school offer?
Two — Grammar (Arts and Sciences) and Commercial (Marketing, Accounting). Both lead to the GCE O-Level.
What are the school hours?
Monday to Friday, 7:30 to 15:30; Saturday morning, 7:30 to 13:30. Sunday is closed.
How does the school support its best performers?
The dean's desk: after the first sequence, students with 15+ averages who pass all papers earn a special outing with the academic deans.
How do I find out about fees?
The fee schedule for the current year is available at the office — one call to 233 322 551 gives the full breakdown.
