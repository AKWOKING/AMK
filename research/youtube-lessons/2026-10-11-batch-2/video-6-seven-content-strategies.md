## 4 · GAP-FILLING

**What the video should have covered and did not:**
- **No mechanics for AEO.** She says "create content for AI" and "prioritise the intersection of
  what AI cares about and where your audience lives," but never says *what to do on Monday*. For a
  small business the realistic actions are narrow and she doesn't list them.
- **No small-business or local case.** Every example is a SaaS, a coach or a creator with a large
  audience. Nothing about a single shop in one city.
- **No measurement.** "Golden hour" and "quality over frequency" are asserted; nothing about how to
  tell whether the change worked.
- **Wikipedia is named as a primary source but is not a realistic target** for a small business —
  notability standards exclude almost every client we will ever have. She doesn't flag that.

**Cameroon / WhatsApp-first / optician application — the realistic AEO action set:**
- **YouTube** is the actionable one of her three: video content with accurate transcripts and
  descriptions is what gets cited (YouTube is ~19% of Google AI Overviews' top-source share,
  per the index above). We already write captions by hand (standing rule, 11/10) — that is exactly
  the artefact AI platforms index.
- **Review platforms** matter for local queries: Perplexity and AI Overviews cite Yelp/G2-type
  sources for recommendation queries (https://searchengineland.com/ai-search-engines-cite-reddit-youtube-and-linkedin-most-study-473138). Our analogue is the **Google Business Profile**, which we
  already work (`sales/FICHE-GOOGLE-PROFILE.md`).
- **The client's own site** must be structured so a machine can extract the facts: address, hours,
  services, ONOC number. That is an existing SEO-checklist concern, not a new one.
- **Wikipedia and Reddit: not actionable** for a Douala optician. Say so plainly rather than
  pretending otherwise.

**Counter-evidence to the video's own framing:** the citation data is drawn from **English-language,
largely US/UK queries**. We have **no data** on whether Cameroonian francophone users query AI
assistants for local services at all, nor on which sources those engines cite for Cameroon. The AEO
strategy is imported wholesale from an English-market evidence base — that limitation is recorded,
not hidden.
