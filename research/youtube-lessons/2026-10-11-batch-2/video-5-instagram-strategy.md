## 4 · GAP-FILLING

**What the video should have covered and did not:**
- **No WhatsApp.** The whole "conversations are the new leads" argument stops inside Instagram DMs.
  For our market the conversation has to end in WhatsApp, and the video never crosses that bridge.
- **No disclosure that the 46% figure is ManyChat's own.** He presents a sponsor's marketing
  statistic as a neutral fact inside a sponsored segment. That is the single most important thing
  this claim audit caught.
- **No cost/benefit on automation.** What it costs in setup, what breaks, what happens to a
  conversation that needs a human.
- **No local-market application.** All examples are US/UK/AU small businesses.

**Cameroon / WhatsApp-first / optician application:** the transferable idea is **"move the CTA into
the conversation."** Concretely for DM Optique: when someone comments on a frame post, the reply is
a DM containing the WhatsApp link with the message pre-written — not "link in bio." That is
**free**, needs no tool, and matches the client's existing `wa.me` pattern. The *principle* is
adopted; the *tool* is not.

**Counter-evidence:** video 6 of this batch says **"prioritise quality over frequency"** and warns
that over-posting trains the algorithm to ignore you. Video 5 is silent on frequency but its
"post at the best times" framing assumes volume. Not a hard contradiction, but a difference of
emphasis we resolve in favour of video 6.
