## 4 · GAP-FILLING

**What the video should have covered and did not:**
- **No measurement at all.** Nothing about what to look at to know whether a post worked. The three
  objectives are asserted, never instrumented.
- **No platform specificity.** It is channel-agnostic to the point of being unusable as a checklist.
- **No cost-of-attention honesty.** "Story inventory" is genuinely good advice but she never says
  how long it takes or how to keep it fresh.
- **No local-business case.** Every example is a personal brand or her own dental-office job.

**Cameroon / WhatsApp-first / optician application — and this is why the video matters to us:**
`content/strategy/CONTENT-STRATEGY-V2-2026-10-05.md` records that our content engine produced
**zero inbound leads**. The three-objectives test is a **diagnostic for exactly that failure**: if
no post can name which of the three jobs it does, the calendar is content for content's sake. For
DM Optique, a post's job is almost always **generation** (a WhatsApp message about an exam or a
frame) — which means the CTA must be a WhatsApp CTA, not "follow us."

**Counter-evidence:** video 6 of this same batch argues **"think in messages, not funnels"** — that
people don't follow your funnel stages. That is a direct tension with organising content by lead
gen / nurture / conversion. Resolution in §5.

## 5 · INTEGRATION RECOMMENDATION

**ADOPT (2 items, both free, both into an existing file — PRE-FLIGHT §4b respected):**
1. **The three-job test.** Before any post is written, it must be able to name its job:
   *generate*, *nurture*, or *convert*. A post that cannot name one does not get made.
   → `content/strategy/CONTENT-STRATEGY-V2-2026-10-05.md`, with a pointer to this batch.
2. **Story inventory.** A standing document of real client stories (a child's first glasses, a
   broken frame repaired, an exam that found something) to draw from instead of inventing content.
   → same file. **Note:** stories must be true and client-consented; the credential-claims rule
   (`content/strategy/RULE-CREDENTIAL-CLAIMS.md`) governs.

**How we resolve the contradiction with video 6:** we keep the **job test** (every post has a job)
and drop the **stage organisation** (we do not file content by funnel stage). A post can carry a
complete message *and* have a job.

**DO NOT ADOPT:** the affiliate tool stack (all paid, all replaceable by work I already do);
know-like-trust and 80/20 as anything we would quote to a client (they are folklore without a
primary source); and any framing that predates 2023 platform behaviour.
