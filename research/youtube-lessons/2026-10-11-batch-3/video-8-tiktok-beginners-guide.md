## 4 · GAP-FILLING

**What the video should have covered and did not:**
- **Nothing about captions or on-screen text.** Batch 1 established that TikTok reads both for
  indexing, and our standing rule (11/10) is that captions are **written by hand** because French
  auto-captions fail on Cameroonian French. A TikTok SEO guide that omits captions is missing the
  layer that matters most for us.
- **No profile optimisation** (username, display name, bio) — the layer batch 1's HubSpot video
  covered properly.
- **No CTA and no conversion path.** It ends at "more views equals more awareness," with no route to
  a customer. For us the route is WhatsApp.
- **No mention of the algorithm trick's risk.** An unverifiable tactic presented to beginners with
  no caveat beyond "apparently."

**Cameroon / WhatsApp-first / optician application:** the **one-month experiment** is the
transferable piece, and it fills a real gap: our content strategy records that the engine produced
**zero inbound leads**, but we have no standing review ritual that would tell us *why*. A fixed
30-day review — which posts earned follows, which earned WhatsApp taps, which earned nothing — is
the cheapest instrument we could add. The 3–5-per-week cadence is **not** adopted; the client
commitment is 2 per week and the review works at any cadence.

**Counter-evidence:** batch 2's video 6 argues **quality over frequency** and warns that algorithms
measure "how many people care," not how often you post. Video 8's 3–5-a-week prescription is not
wrong, but it is not the lever; the *review* is.

## 5 · INTEGRATION RECOMMENDATION

**ADOPT (1 item, free, into an existing file — §4b respected):**
- **The one-month review.** At a fixed 30-day point, read the account's analytics and answer three
  questions: which posts earned **follows**, which earned **WhatsApp taps**, which earned nothing —
  then make more of the first and stop making the third. Cadence-independent; works at 2/week.
  → `content/strategy/TIKTOK-SEO-CHECKLIST-2026-10.md` §D, with a pointer to this batch.

**DO NOT ADOPT:**

  exactly the kind of claim our standing rule forbids us to repeat. If it worked, it would also be
  the kind of manipulation platforms remove.
- **The 3–5-videos-a-week cadence** as a client recommendation — contradicts our 2/week commitment
  and batch 2's quality-over-frequency finding.
- **Primal Video PLUS and the gear links** — paid, and the free method is already captured.
