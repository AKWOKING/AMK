**Method note (corrected 2026-10-11).** An earlier version of this file said transcripts were not
retrievable in this sandbox. **That was wrong.** The path documented in `research/YouTube-Lessons.md`
§0 works: `https://youtubetotranscript.com/transcript?v=VIDEO_ID` via `fetch_page`. All three
transcripts were retrieved **in full** (video 1: 4/4 chunks · video 2: 5/5 · video 3: 2/2) and the
reports are written from them.

What the transcript service does **not** expose: video **duration, publication date and view
count**. Those are marked UNVERIFIED in each report rather than guessed. Subscriber counts are
visible on the transcript page and are cited. Every factual claim is marked VERIFIED /
UNVERIFIED / FALSE / VENDOR SELF-REPORT; nothing is invented.
