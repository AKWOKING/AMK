## 4 · GAP-FILLING

**What the video should have covered and did not:**
- **No cadence, measurement or conversion.** It is purely a profile/content optimisation
  checklist; nothing about how often to post, what to measure, or how a view becomes a customer.
- **No warning about keyword stuffing** in captions — independent guides flag this explicitly.
- **No business-vs-personal account distinction**, which changes available features and analytics.
- **No non-English / non-US application.** Every example is US English.

**Cameroon / WhatsApp-first / optician application (the strongest transfer of the batch):**
- Target queries in French: « opticien Douala », « lunettes Douala », « examen de vue Bonabéri »,
  « montures Douala ».
- **Bio (80 chars, French):** what we do + who we help + WhatsApp CTA.
- **Location tag** = Bonabéri / Douala, plus the city as a keyword in bio and captions.
- **On-screen keyword text in the first 3 seconds**, spoken keyword, auto-captions corrected (our
  videos will be in French; TikTok's transcription of French needs a manual check).
- **Hashtags:** 2–3 broad/local (#Douala #Cameroun), 2–3 niche (#OpticienDouala #ExamenDeVue),
  1 branded (#DMOptique).
- **CTA must be WhatsApp**, and the bio link must be the client's site (never a bare number).

**Counter-evidence to the video's premise:** while 49% of US consumers have *used* TikTok as a
search engine, only **4% of Gen Z say they are more likely to rely on TikTok over Google** in 2026
(down from 8% in 2024, Adobe), and Google remains the primary starting point for 46% of 18–24s.
So TikTok SEO is worth doing as a **supplement**, not as a replacement for being findable on
Google — which is exactly where our client sites already play.
