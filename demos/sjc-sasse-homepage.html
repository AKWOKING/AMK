Who we are

A legacy built since 1939

St. Joseph's College Sasse is a Catholic secondary school in the South-West Region of Cameroon. From its first classrooms to today, our mission has never changed: academic rigor, moral formation and the habit of excellence — preparing young men for national and global responsibilities.

Strongly supported by the SOBA alumni network — which awarded FCFA 23 million in scholarships and teacher bonuses this year alone — Sasse continues to raise the bar, aiming for perfect A grades across the board.

“We are made of excellence”

Discipline. Hard work. Integrity. The Sasse tradition of forming leaders is lived every day in our classrooms, dormitories and grounds.

— The Sasse Promise
Academics

Programs from First Cycle to Sixth Form

First Cycle

Form 1 – Form 3

A solid foundation in the core subjects — English, French, Mathematics, Sciences and Humanities — with individual follow-up for every student.

Second Cycle

Form 4 – Form 5

Preparation for the GCE Ordinary Level with rigorous past-paper practice, laboratory work and mock examinations.

Sixth Form

Lower & Upper Sixth — Arts and Sciences

Intensive GCE Advanced Level preparation in Sciences and Arts streams, targeting top grades at the national examination.
