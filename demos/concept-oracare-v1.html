FAQ

Questions Patients Ask

I'm anxious about the dentist. How do you help?
Tell us when you book — we adjust the pace, explain every step before it happens, and you can signal us to pause at any time. Anxiety is part of the visit, not a failure.
Do you handle emergencies?
Yes — same-day emergency slots are kept for pain and injury. Call the number on this page and ask for the emergency slot.
Are you bilingual?
Yes — we see patients in English and French, and this website is in both languages.
How much does treatment cost?
Every plan comes with a clear written quote before we start — no surprises. Ask for a quote at your first visit, it's free.
