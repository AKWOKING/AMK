008Quick Answers

The questions we get most

Will it hurt?+

Most patients are surprised — the first visit is usually the least intense part. For anything like fillings, we use local anesthesia, and we explain every step before we start it.

How do I pay?+

Cash, MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money. The price you're quoted before we begin is the price you pay — no surprises at the end.

How soon can I be seen?+

Send us a WhatsApp message — most patients are booked within a few days, and emergencies are seen as soon as possible.

I'm not sure what I need. Where do I start?+

Start with a general consultation. We examine, explain what's going on, and give you a written plan with clear prices before any treatment.

Where exactly are you?+

Inside St. Pius Hospital, Mayor's Street, Molyko, Buea. Use the Get Directions button above and we'll see you at the reception.
