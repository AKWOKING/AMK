Nos sections

Trois voies, une même exigence de travail

Families choose the path that fits their child; all three share the same discipline, bilingual environment and supervised study.

Le tronc commun
GÉ

Section générale

Le parcours secondaire classique et bilingue de la 6e à la Terminale, menant aux examens officiels (BEPC, Probatoire, Baccalauréat ; GCE dans le parcours anglo-saxon).

Section technique

Hands-on technical and commercial pathways for pupils headed toward skilled trades and technical examinations.

Section anglo-saxonne

English-medium Forms 1 to Upper Sixth, leading to the GCE O and A Levels.

Centre de documentation

A reading and reference room supporting study across all three sections.

Salle multimédia

Computer and multimedia facilities for practical digital work.

Laboratoire de langues

Dedicated practice for the bilingual English and French programme.

La pension

La pension comprise, de la première à la Terminale

COMOBIL's boarding (pension) is included in the fees from 6e to Terminale. Pupils live, study and take their meals on one campus, with supervised study and daily structure.

  • ✓Boarding included in the published fees (the full schedule is provided by the administration)
  • ✓One supervised campus from study time to lights out
  • ✓Infirmary on the school grounds

Le Groupe WAFO

One educational family in Douala

COMOBIL (Les Lauréats) belongs to the WAFO educational group, which runs five establishments and celebrates pupils each year at the Excellence Académique awards through the L'Enfance Joyeuse du Cameroun association.

Les questions des parents

Avant de contacter le secrétariat

Quelles sections propose le COMOBIL ?
Three: the general section, the technical section and the Anglo-Saxon (English, GCE) section, within one school complex.
La pension est-elle comprise ?
Yes. Boarding (pension) is included in the fees from 6e to Terminale. The administration provides the current fee schedule and the boarding list on request.
Où se trouve l'école ?
At the Bonamoussadi market roundabout in Douala, B.P. 6081. The administration line is 233 470 608 and the email is comobil@yahoo.fr.
À quel âge ou niveau un enfant peut-il entrer ?
The complex admits from the first year of secondary (6e / Form 1) through to Terminale / Upper Sixth. The office confirms the entry requirements for each section.
Quels autres établissements appartiennent au groupe ?
The WAFO group runs five establishments: COMOBIL Les Lauréats, Collège La Maturité, Groupe Scolaire WAFO, EPL Fraternité and ENIEG WAFO.
