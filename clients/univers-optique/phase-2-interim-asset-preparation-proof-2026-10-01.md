## What the proof demonstrates

- Each candidate has a transparent PNG derivative.
- The two lens interiors are transparent, so a later customer-photo overlay can show through.
- The visible stock labels, printed lens markings and rear temples were excluded where the mask permitted.
- Each asset is mapped to its original photograph, has provisional visual lens-centre coordinates and is marked as requiring inventory confirmation.

## Known limitations to review

These are technical proof assets, not final catalogue assets. Several derivatives still contain pale table/background shadow pixels or bright reflections around portions of the outer edge. The source photos also have small perspective differences. The manifest records those limitations rather than presenting the assets as production-ready.
