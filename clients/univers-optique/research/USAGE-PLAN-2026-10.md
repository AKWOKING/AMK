# USAGE PLAN — UNIVERS OPTIQUE TRY-ON TOOL · 03/10 · working document

> Client-facing (EN). FR on request. **In-shop only: the tool is a live sales aid used at the counter,
> in front of the client. No WhatsApp routing, no remote channel, no lead capture — the client is
> physically present.** This plan is what makes the tool different from the three prior websites:
> it changes what happens during the sale, and we measure it.

## The three moments at the counter
1. **Hook (10 s).** « Would you like to see how they look on you — in one minute, without trying
   anything on? » One photo, and the frames appear on the client's face.
2. **Exploration (2–4 min).** The client swipes. Rule of thumb: let them lead; the collaborator names
   what they see (« that shape suits your face »). Every frame shown costs nothing to handle, clean
   or adjust.
3. **Close.** The client points at what they like; those 2–3 frames come off the shelf for the real
   try-on. Virtual narrows, physical confirms, the sale closes.

## What the tool measures (in-shop metrics only — a simple monthly sheet, no analytics backend)
| Metric | Baseline (physical only) | Target with the tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Frames tried per client** | 3–5 (handling limits it) | **15–20 swiped**, 2–3 tried physically |
| **Time per interaction** | — (measure week 1) | tracked, trend matters more than absolute |
| **Conversion rate** | sessions → sales, week 0 | **higher** — more frames seen = more informed choice |
| **Revenue per session** | — (measure week 0) | **higher** — the tool shows higher-value frames without effort |

## How it gets measured (no server, no app analytics — privacy kept)
- **Week 0 (before launch):** one week of baseline on a paper/phone sheet — clients served, frames
  tried, sales, revenue. That's the only honest comparison point.
- **From launch:** same sheet, one line per session: swiped / physically tried / sold / amount.
  2 minutes a day, filled by whoever used the tool.
- **Monthly read (with AMK):** frames-tried trend, conversion, revenue per session — the numbers that
  go into the case study (anonymised) and into his own management.

## What the tool never does
No client data leaves the device · no photo is uploaded · no account, no login · nothing a
competitor can browse. The metrics sheet is his, in his shop, on his terms.
