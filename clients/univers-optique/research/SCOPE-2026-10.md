## Context
Following the conversation of 27 September and Dr. Bayang's agreement to collaborate, this document
formalises the scope and price of the try-on tool. One-time build. No retainer.

## What is built (all inside the one-time fee)
- **Local-only Android app, sideloaded** — no Play Store, no public URL, nothing hosted.
- **AR try-on at the counter:** one photo of the client → on-device face landmarks (MediaPipe) →
  frame overlay → swipe through frames, one by one.
- **Up to 3 devices** — his phone and his collaborators'. **Tablet and smartphone both work.**
- **Frame catalog: up to 30 frames in the MVP**, photographed locally, stored on the device.
  Nothing is ever uploaded. No backend.
- **Self-service catalog admin:** add, remove or reorder frames directly in the app (local import) —
  Dr. Bayang or a collaborator does this alone, without contacting us. This is a feature, not a
  service: once built, he owns it.
- **No WhatsApp routing, no lead capture, no public-facing anything.**

## Not included
More than 3 devices · more than 30 frames in the initial catalog · multi-shop sync · hosted catalog
updates · Play Store distribution · ongoing changes without a new quote.

## Updates & maintenance
- **Catalog updates: self-service** (see above). No dependency on us for routine changes.
- **App updates (new version):** delivered as a new APK, sideloaded — reinstall, minutes.
- **Warranty: bug fixes free for 30 days after delivery.** After that: ad-hoc — he calls, we quote,
  he decides. No monthly fee: the app is offline, nothing on a server can break, nothing is licensed
  monthly. A retainer on this build would be a padded line.
