## Design Read
Dials: **Soft 5/10 · Editorial 7/10 · Dense 3/10** → boutique, not clinic. Direction **« LE MIROIR »**: warm ink-teal/amber/clay on cream, Fraunces serif display, the fitting-room metaphor, and a **frame-shape selector** as the one memorable interaction. Ties to the client: their trade is equal parts medical check and personal style — most local optician sites only speak the first language.
