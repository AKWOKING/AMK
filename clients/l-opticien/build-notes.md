## 2 · What the concept answers (from the sweep dossier)
Facebook-only presence, no site: a client searching « opticien Bali/Douala » finds a page, not a shop. No price context, no way to book an eye test, no frame catalogue, no repair request path.
**Concept answers:** eye-check booking on WhatsApp · **frame-shape selector** (the signature interaction) · honest quote builder (lens type / who / use → WhatsApp) · repairs & adjustments · children · sunglasses · FAQ · EN|FR · sticky WhatsApp.

## 3 · Direction explored (§19.3) and rejected alternatives
- **« LE MIROIR » (retained)** — warm editorial boutique: ink-teal `#0E3B43` + amber `#F0A03C` + clay `#C96F4A` on cream, **Fraunces** display + Inter, the fitting-room metaphor (« Voir net. Se voir bien. »), shape selector as the centrepiece.
- *Rejected 1 — « OPTIQUE CLINIQUE »* (white/blue medical): confuses an optician with a clinic and erases the fashion half of the trade.
- *Rejected 2 — « LUNETTES D'ABORD »* (photo-wall e-commerce grid): implies an online shop with stock and delivery we cannot promise in Douala; creates a support burden instead of a booking.

## 4 · Uniqueness (registry check)
Distinct from every existing AMK concept: `site/mitoc.html` (named MITOC concept, royal blue `#15539E` + Outfit + different structure), lab concept (cyan/navy + Space Grotesk), dental trio, school trio. **6 axes differ**: layout (selector-led), palette (ink/amber/clay), type (Fraunces), imagery (warm boutique), section order (shape → shop → quote), tone (editorial).

## 5 · Accuracy discipline
**No prices anywhere** — the quote is built by the optician (the lesson from every Cameroonian optician site we checked: they all hide prices, and inventing them would be a fatal claim). No brands. No « dépistage gratuit » claim (that is Golden Eyes' offer, not L'Opticien's). WhatsApp number shown as a **placeholder** (`+237 6XX…`) — the real line replaces it before sharing. Photos are generated illustrative images, labelled.
