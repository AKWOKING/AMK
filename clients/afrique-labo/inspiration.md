## 2 · Design Read (three dials + direction)

- **Dials:** *Soft* 3/10 · *Editorial* 6/10 · *Dense* 4/10 → **precision-clinical, not soft wellness.**
- **Direction taken (of three explored, §19.3 — see build-notes):** **"FEUILLE DE RÉSULTAT"** — the page borrows the anatomy of a lab report: monospaced micro-labels, numbered sections, hairline rules, a reference column, and one big price/test console. Cyan is the *signal* colour (their poster); navy is the *structure*; red appears only where urgency is real (24h, urgences).
- **How it ties to Afrique Labo:** their problem is **opacity** (dead domain, template site with a "Your logo" placeholder, prices locked inside a printed grid and a WhatsApp catalogue). The design therefore makes **the catalogue and the practical answers the hero content** — a patient can find a test, see the price, and send the request in two taps.
