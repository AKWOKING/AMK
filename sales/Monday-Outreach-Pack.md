# SALES OS — the 4-video engine (full play: `AMK-Sales-Playbook-v2.md`)
**NO COLD CALLS (King, 13 Sep):** text / WhatsApp / Messenger / email only. A call happens only if the prospect themselves proposes it.
**"GETS THE JOB DONE" FILTER (King, 13 Sep):** if a lead's site is current and internally maintained, the buyer believes it works → slow or no buyer. D/E (broken/expired/absent) = gold; "internally fixable" decay (old number, dead link) = weak pitch. Park such leads WITH revival triggers; if pitching them ever, lead with conversion (admission flow), never repair.
1. **1-hour reply rule** (09:00–21:00, 7 days) — speed-to-lead is the #1 conversion lever.
2. **Pull it up** — they pick Thursday → "I actually have a slot tomorrow 15:00 — does that work?"
3. **BAMFAM** — no conversation ends without a scheduled next step ("I'll send the preview Tue 09:00 — I'll ping you when it's out").
4. **Kill list** — COMOBIL + OraCare (the two 18s) + any fresh "yes" get daily visible attention.
5. **Referral — "Who do you know that's like you?"** — ask at concept handover AND at close (referrals close at 80–90%).
6. **Prep = 10 min/call** — one personalized opening fact from the Deep-Dive; they must feel *known* in minute 1.
7. **Listen 2× — answer questions with questions.** Discovery trio (schools): registrations last term? / what do parents ask most? / when's next registration? (clinics): how do new patients find you? / what do they ask before coming? / emergency slots?
8. **Drill 15 min/day** (30-sec open, price+pause, 5 flips) — with a friend or voice memo, NEVER on real leads.
9. **Zombie check before price:** who else decides (spouse/co-director/bishop)? · what's the worry? play it out. · **Price + PAUSE:** "…100 000 francs, half to start." then silence. Never discount — trade scope/timing.
10. **The Cardone question** after every preview walkthrough: "Have you heard enough to make a decision — or is there one thing I can still show you?" / "Assez entendu pour décider — ou y a-t-il une chose que je peux encore vous montrer ?"
11. **One message ≤ 5 lines.** Concise beats impressive.

**Pain ladder (verified facts only, across the conversation — never one message):** latent ("your page is doing well, right?") → realized ("what comes up first when a parent Googles [school]?" → show the verified dead domain/template/404) → extreme, one sentence on the call ("every open day, some parents have already searched — right now the first result is [X]").
**Enemy (never a named school):** the template site with another school's name · the expired domain that looks like the school died · "we have a page, so we're fine."

**THE CALL RUN-SHEET (when they accept the preview):** prep 10 min → 30-sec open w/ personalized fact + micro-agreement ("Can I show you something in 10 minutes?") → let them scroll, you listen → one verified pain screenshot → future-pace the open-day scene (EN: "A parent walks in and shows their phone: 'I already saw your site — the results, the dorms, the form. I'm registering my brother's child.'") → zombie check → price + pause → Cardone question → if yes: 50/50 + agree exact day for MoMo ref (BAMFAM) → **referral ask** → if no: schedule M+2, log objection verbatim.
