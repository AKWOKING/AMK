## Models worth stealing from (global)

- **"Work on big ideas without the busy work."** (Asana) — core message + why care, one sentence.
- **"Turn emails into revenue."** (Mailchimp) — benefit verb + revenue noun, no feature.
- **"A thousand songs in your pocket."** (Apple iPod) — metonymy; visualizable; nobody else could say it.
- **"The sport sedan for people who inherited brains instead of wealth."** — makes the customer feel smart.
- **"Worn by supermodels in London and dads in Ohio."** (New Balance) — visual, falsifiable, only they can say it.
- **"They don't write songs about Volvos."** (Corvette) — enemy + category jab in six words.
- **"I've never read The Economist." — Management trainee, aged 42.** — a story in 9 words; no logo, no image; the ad *is* the line.
- **"Your car has five numbers on the speedometer. Volvo has six."** — comparison that can't be made by anyone else; text aligns with visual.
- **"How can you make two months' salary last forever?"** (De Beers) — impossibility + contrast, and the answer sells the ring.
- **"Couch to 5K."** — the zoom-in drill's finished product: from "regain fitness" to a concrete object.
