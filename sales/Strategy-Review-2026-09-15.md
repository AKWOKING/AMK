## 1 · Market review (Cameroon, not Las Vegas)

### What the five videos assume that does NOT apply
Cards/Stripe subscriptions · email-first buying · businesses ranking on Google
Maps at US density · $297/mo SaaS stacks · paid ads funnels. In Cameroon the
discovery stack is **WhatsApp → Facebook → TikTok → word of mouth → radio/banners**,
money is **cash/Mobile Money termly**, and Google usage exists mainly for
**clinic/lab/pharmacy searches** (Solidarity's 54 Google reviews prove it).

### What DOES apply, confirmed locally
1. **Gifting the build beats asking permission to pitch** (Erhart) — already our
   model (free preview, mockup image first); his data validates it.
2. **Local, one geography at a time** (Darrel) — Buea quarters, one boda loop;
   expand deliberately to Douala (§6).
3. **Recurring care revenue is the real business** (all five) — and Cameroon
   already pays for it (below).
4. **Outcomes, not pages** (Zoho) — admissions/enquiries/bookings.

### Local price map (researched 15 Sep) — our 100 000 is well positioned
| Provider | Vitrine (one-page/small) | Maintenance/mo |
|---|---|---|
| Krexora, Douala (premium) | **from 450 000**; e-commerce from 950 000 | **from 80 000/mo** |
| HuginMboa, Douala (studio) | **from ~200 000** | quoted on request |
| Webmaster-Freelance.cm, Yaoundé ("Opération visibilité") | **from 150 000** | — |
| Camerbiz marketplace package | 100 000/**yr** incl. domain+hosting, 5 pages | bundled |
| afribobo marketplace kids | 20 000–130 000 (templates, no bilingual/WA design) | none |
| **AMK founding offer** | **100 000 once, bilingual EN|FR, WA-first, 3G-fast, named concept, live 3–5 days** | proposed 10k/25k mo (addendum §4) |

Reading: credible Cameroon studios charge **150–450 k**; the credible floor for a
*hygiene* package is ~100 k/year. AMK's founding 100 k is the cheapest *serious*
offer on the market and visibly above the 20–35 k template kids — **defensible,
not cheap-looking, and the Care price of 10–25 k/month is far below Krexora's 80 k
anchor**. Objection flip for the playbook: *"Douala agencies start at 150 000 for
a French-only vitrine; the 100 000 founding price is the two-slot founding price,
then it aligns with the market."*

### Seasonality (acts now, not later)
- **Schools:** late registration runs into **October** (Summerset's principal told
  Cameroon Tribune parents are pushing arrivals to October) — the admissions pain
  is live for the next ~4 weeks, then the sales window largely closes until Dec/Jan.
- **Clinics:** year-round; no seasonality argument against prioritising them now.

## 2 · ICP review — who actually signs

### Tier 1 — hunt now (OWNER-BUYER, money + motive + no site)
1. **Private clinics, labs, optical/dental with a Google profile carrying reviews
   (10+) and NO website link.** Reviews = proven Google demand; no site = leak;
   daily cash = ability to pay 100 k without a board meeting (Solidarity shape,
   MITOC shape). Highest-fit class in the whole funnel.
2. **WhatsApp-Business/catalogue owners with no Google presence** (MITOC shape):
   already digital, immediate grasp of the offer, cheap to convince.
3. **Boarding / bilingual fee-paying colleges, 100+ GCE candidates, no site,
   lay proprietor** — **refined by King 15 Sep evening (criteria below, §10 D4):**
   exclusively **lay-private**, **old enough to have parent trust, GCE history
   and an active online presence** (live Facebook page, parent engagement),
   **young enough still to need the boost and lack a site**; **highest bonus =
   ever ran Facebook ads**. (St Theresa, Summerset, Baird, NABESK shapes.)
   Out-of-town/diaspora parents ARE on Google; one extra boarder
   (180 k+/term) pays for the site twice. Every school build now includes the
   new **Student Corner** (results honour roll, downloads, research links,
   shareable news — §10 D4).

### Tier 2 — opportunistic
Small lay day schools with active Facebook admissions · hotels/pharmacies/larger
cabinet médicines found on Maps sweeps · Douala owner-buyers reachable fully by
WhatsApp (remote delivery).

### Tier 3 — do not spend touches (standing rule, reaffirmed)
Missionary/diocesan/PCC/Baptist/government (BOARD-BUYER) · anyone with a decent
maintained site (e.g. Didacweb-built COSBINAL, Liwo, Soliman, St Veronica) ·
personal numbers of staff (no end-runs) · disputed ownership (Inter Comp).

**Scoring add for the CRM** (from Darrel's sheet): `Gatekeeper name` +
`Google reviews (n)` + `WA Business? yes/no` + `website link on Google? yes/no`.
Reviews+no-link+WA-Business = A+; sweep these first.

## 3 · Offer review

**Keep (data-backed):** 100 000 FCFA founding, 50/50, two slots, never discount;
bilingual standard; live 3–5 days; named 24 h preview; domain registered in
client's name; demo WA routing until final payment.

**Sharpen — the way it is SOLD (Zoho/Erhart lesson), not the price:**
- Lead every conversation with the one outcome: **schools** = more admission
  WhatsApp + fewer fee phone-calls; **clinics** = bookings + price trust before
  arrival. Scripts already drafted (addendum §2).
- Lead with the ROI sentence: *"One new boarder this term is worth more than the
  site costs for the year"* / *"a handful of bookings a month does the same."*

**Add 1 — Named-first gifting for a hot list of 5 (the single biggest conversion
lever in the videos; King already does this instinctively for OraCare/MITOC).**
Pre-build (60–90 min each with the builders) a **semi-named** concept for the five
best OWNER-BUYER targets — their real crest/name colours pulled from their FB page,
their quarter, their fees marked DEMO — and message: *"I went ahead and drafted
yours — may I send the link?"* This is Erhart's gift mechanic at full strength
while keeping accuracy law (no invented facts; DEMO tags on everything). Requires
King's approval; candidates in §6.

**Add 2 — "Digital Front Door" wedge for clinics (foot-in where a website feels
big).** Bundle, free with every founding site, and usable as a standalone small
offer: **claim & clean the Google Business Profile** (Solidarity's profile is
literally unclaimed/stale on medicoor; MITOC has none), hours, photos, services,
WA link, and a printed "review us" card. It costs minutes, delivers visible
outcome immediately, and is the natural first Care task. It mirrors what the US
videos call "systems around the site" without any SaaS bill.

**Add 3 — Care Plan (addendum §4) stays a POST-founder offer**, benchmarked now
against Krexora's 80 k/mo: Basic 100 k/yr (≈8,3 k/mo effective — consider pricing
at **120 k/yr or 10 k/mo equivalent, annual MoMo**) and Growth 250 k/yr. Only
pitch at the 30-day check-in with the first monthly report in hand.

**Do not add:** login portals, email automation, US stacks, e-commerce scope for
schools (scope creep kills the 3–5 day promise and the margin).

## 7 · Decision gates (write these before emotions do)

- **Sun 20:** if qualified touches ≥ 15 and replies ≥ 3 → on plan, continue.
  If touches < 15 → the failure is volume/activity, not offer: fix the calendar.
- **Wed 23:** if touches ≥ 25 with reply rate < 10% → the MESSAGE/channel is the
  problem: switch default outreach to named-first gifts and double inbound content.
  If replies ≥ 10% but no walkthroughs → preview/ask is the problem; rehearse the
  handoff. If walkthroughs but no close → price/offer objection is the problem;
  adjust scope/terms (never price) and bring Care framing.
- **30 Sep:** if 0 closes after ≥ 35 complete-funnel touches, full ICP/offer reset
  (including the Front-Door standalone price and Douala-first weighting). Not before.
