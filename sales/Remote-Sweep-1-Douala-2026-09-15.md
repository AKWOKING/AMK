## D · Competitor intelligence from the sweep

- **mondocteur237.com** already aggregates Douala doctors with 15 000 FCFA
  booking and WhatsApp confirmations — the market is being educated to book
  online by others, for free. Individual clinics/labs still lack their own
  front; our pitch is "own the patient relationship, don't rent it from a
  platform".
- **Shine Dental runs aggressive SEO** ("top 10 cabinets" listicles, city
  landing pages) — proof the dental niche searches online in Douala; also a
  model to learn topic clusters from, never a target.
- DoualaTour "WhatsApp click" counts (159–292 per profile) are third-party
  evidence WA is the chosen action channel — cite carefully, never invent.

## E · Process for each verified hot-lister (named-first gift, D2 approved)

   Google + Facebook (accuracy law: prices stay DEMO unless published).
2. 60–90 min: adapt `site/build_sample_clinic.py` (or optical/school as
   relevant) → semi-named concept on a throwaway Vercel URL, private.
3. Attach `demos/shots/mockup-clinic-wa.jpg`? No — for NAMED gifts capture
   the named concept's own mockup; msg 1 stays ≤5 lines, image first:
   *"Bonjour, I help clinics/labs in Douala turn Google searches into
   WhatsApp bookings. While researching [quarter] I drafted a bilingual
   concept for [name] — may I send the link? Every price is a placeholder
   until you approve."* (FR version in addendum §1.)
4. On yes: link, then invite-choice (10-min WhatsApp call vs full async
   walkthrough). Silence: M+2/M+4/M+7 then stop.
