# Playbook Addendum #2 — SEO in the AI era, translated to AMK (15 Sep 2026 evening)

Study: Surfer Academy, *The Complete SEO & AI SEO Course for 2026* (50 min,
Matt Kenyon) and Ahrefs, *SEO in 2026: How I'd Rank in Google in the AI Era*
(Sam Oh). Applied to Cameroon: what changes for client sites, what is in
every build for free, and what stays inside the Growth Care Plan.

## 1 · The three shifts that matter to us

1. **AI Overviews eat informational clicks (top result loses ~58% when an
   overview appears — Ahrefs data), but they cannot transact.** Queries that
   require an action — *"lab near me", "scan price", "book appointment",
   "boarding school Buea fees", "GCE results [school]"* — still send clicks.
   **Every client page must target action queries with a visible WhatsApp
   action, not blog-style information.**
2. **Discovery is multi-surface and brand-driven.** Buyers hop Google →
   YouTube → Facebook/reviews → WhatsApp; AI assistants cite YouTube,
   review/niche and regional sites heavily; **branded mentions correlate
   with AI visibility more than backlinks.** Consequences for AMK:
   - client sites get the Google Business Profile + reviews + consistent
     NAP (name/address/phone) treatment from day one (the free front-door);
   - AMK mirrors its TikTok videos to **YouTube Shorts** (Google's AI
     Overviews favour YouTube) and keeps the AMK Facebook Page identical;
   - every launched client becomes a named portfolio mention (with consent)
     and a directory listing (Cameroon business directories, Google Maps).
3. **Traditional SEO still wins exactly where our clients play: local,
   transactional, narrow.** Long-tail, city+niche keywords are low
   competition; new sites start there (Surfer's "keyword sweet spot":
   demand × fit × intent × difficulty). We never promise rankings; we build
   the structure that captures the clicks AI cannot.

## 2 · Keyword map — the pages clients actually need (EN + FR seeds)

**Clinics / labs / optical / dental** (money = booking & price trust):
- `<service> price <city>`: "prix échographie Douala", "ultrasound scan
  price Buea", "prix bilans sanguins laboratoire <quartier>", "HIV test
  price", "PCR test <city>"
- `<service> near me / ouvert`: "laboratoire ouvert samedi <city>",
  "cabinet médical <quartier>", "pharmacy de garde" (adjacency),
  "opticien <city>"
- transactional: "prendre rendez-vous médecin <city>", "laboratoire
  résultats en ligne"
- cluster pages (Growth, 2/term): one service explained + price + WhatsApp
  CTA per page (e.g. antenatal scans package, dental cleaning, optical
  frames + eye tests).
**Schools** (money = admissions & boarding trust):
- "boarding secondary school <city> fees", "collège privé internat <ville>
  frais", "bilingual college Buea", "GCE results <school name>"
- "école bilingue <quartier> frais de scolarité", "inscription
  sixième <ville>"
- cluster pages: one per programme/boarding/fees/results + the **Student
  Corner** (results-day honour roll, downloads, official links — built).
Rule from Surfer: **one money page first (homepage with WA CTA), then one
topic cluster at a time.** No 20-page sites at founding scope; pages above
the one-pager are Growth Care work.

## 3 · Free-in-every-build SEO checklist (add to launch QA)

Already true in the builders; now verified per launch:
- [ ] `<title>` ≤ 60 chars, keyword front-loaded:
  "Cabinet médical & laboratoire · Bonabéri, Douala | RDV WhatsApp"; one
  H1; meta description with city + WhatsApp; canonical present.
- [ ] schema.org JSON-LD (MedicalClinic/LocalBusiness/School), name, phone,
  city, hours, sameAs Facebook.
- [ ] sitemap.xml + robots.txt (exists in samples); bilingual hreflang-free
  toggle acceptable for v1 (single URL `?lang=fr`).
- [ ] every `<img>` has descriptive alt text; page < ~1 MB above the fold;
  3G load checked (our speed promise is a ranking + UX claim).
- [ ] FAQ section with real questions (Q&A schema candidate for v2);
      prices/fees written as text (scanners and AI both read it).
- [ ] WhatsApp button above the fold and in the mobile bar; contact
      details identical to the Google profile.
- [ ] At launch (AMK action, ~20 min, owner only clicks verification):
      Google Business Profile claimed/filled, **Google Search Console +
      Bing Webmaster Tools** verified (ChatGPT/Bing index), sitemap
      submitted, 8–12 real photos uploaded; review QR card printed.
- [ ] No claims of ranking, no paid ads, no keyword stuffing; city names
      only where true (accuracy law).
Tools needed: none paid. GSC + Bing + the free Screaming Frog 500-URL tier
for quarterly audits inside Care. No Surfer/Ahrefs subscriptions at our
scale; manual top-5 SERP reads per cluster page.

## 4 · Growth Care delivery now defined by the courses

- 2 topic pages/term, each: read top-5 Google results → cover table stakes
  + one gap (real local prices, real hours, our client's actual service),
  title/meta/internal links to the money page, WhatsApp C.
- Monthly report adds: GSC impressions/queries (rising impressions before
  clicks is the early win signal), GBP views/calls/WA taps, review count.
- Quarterly: one review-reply coaching session, new photos, one cluster
  audit, directory/portfolio mention check.
- Results-day play for schools (annual traffic spike): honour roll + pass
  PDF published same day, parent-share prompt, link to camgceb.org.

## 5 · AMK's own acquisition SEO (small, compounding, no spend)

- Repost every vertical TikTok/IG video to **YouTube Shorts** with title
  pattern: "Site web bilingue pour clinique à Douala — RDV WhatsApp |
  AMK" / "Bilingual clinic website in Buea, Cameroon"; descriptions carry
  the city+niche keywords and the wa.me link.
- amk-cm.vercel.app (later own domain): one money page per niche
  (clinics, schools) with the demos; later one cluster page per city.
- Directory/brand mentions: free Cameroon business listing sites,
  LinkedIn/FB Page About sections, Google Business Profile for AMK itself;
  every live client links "Website by AMK" in the footer (already in the
  concepts) — a real, relevant, local backlink each.
- The 60–90 s handoff recordings and client testimonials (with consent)
  become Shorts: review/result content is what AI and YouTube cite.

## 6 · Rejects / non-adoptions

No paid SEO retainers until Care exists with delivered pages · no ranking
promises or "page 1 in a week" · no mass AI content (Surfer: 96% of pages
get zero traffic; the bar is 5× better, and in a tiny bilingual niche
hand-built pages win) · no rank-tracker SaaS spend · no blog farms ·
GDPR/cookie banners still out · portals/email automation still out.
