## 2 · Outcome lines per niche (use in REPLIES and walkthroughs — never in msg 1, which stays ≤5 lines, no selling)

The videos' shared thesis: buyers don't buy pages, they buy more customers / fewer
headaches. Our templates already deliver these; the upgrade is naming ONE metric
the buyer cares about and tying every feature to it.

**Schools (proprietor/principal):**
- EN: "The site's job is one thing: **more admission enquiries on your WhatsApp, and fewer phone calls asking 'how much are your fees?'** Fees and the admission steps answer themselves; parents can enrol at 10 p.m. when they're actually scrolling."
- FR: "Le site fait une seule chose : **plus de demandes d'inscription sur votre WhatsApp, et moins d'appels pour demander les frais.** Les frais et les démarches se lisent seuls ; un parent peut inscrire son enfant à 22 h, au moment où il regarde son téléphone."
- Three pillars in order: **admissions (acquisition) → fees visible (fewer haggling/time-waster calls) → results/reputation (GCE pages win the comparison search).**

**Clinics/labs/opticians (owner/medical director):**
- EN: "Patients already Google before they choose. The site gives them **the price before they arrive, the WhatsApp booking button, and your laboratory on the map in two languages** — then every question that used to tie up your front desk arrives as a written WhatsApp message."
- FR: "Les patients cherchent sur Google avant de choisir. Le site leur donne **le prix avant d'arriver, le bouton WhatsApp pour prendre rendez-vous, et votre laboratoire sur la carte en deux langues** — les questions qui occupaient la réception arrivent maintenant par écrit sur WhatsApp."
- Three pillars: **bookings (acquisition) → transparent FCFA prices (trust/arrival conversion) → results on WhatsApp + reviews (retention/reputation).**

Discovery questions to quantify the outcome before quoting Growth (from Playbook A7, expanded):
- School: "How many admission messages do you get in a normal week today? How many calls are just people asking fees? When does registration peak?"
- Clinic: "How do most new patients find you now? How many call to ask prices before coming? How many no-shows per week?"
Record the number. After 60 days live, compare in the monthly report — the comparison is the renewal and the referral.

## 4 · Care Plan (retainer) proposal — King decides; nothing changes before he signs off

All five videos monetize **monthly recurring revenue**, not the build. Our
reality: no Stripe, cards rare, but Mobile Money works and clients already pay
school fees termly. Therefore: **annual or quarterly prepay, never monthly card.**

**Proposal — offer Care Plans only AFTER the two founding clients are live (post-30 Sep design):**

| | **Basic Care — 10 000 FCFA/mo** | **Growth Care — 25 000 FCFA/mo** |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting + domain renewal + SSL + uptime watch | ✅ | ✅ |
| Small text/price/photo edits (WhatsApp screenshot request) | 2/month, 48 h | unlimited same-week |
| Monthly 1-page report (uptime, page views, WA button taps/refs) | ✅ | ✅ |
| Termly / seasonal content refresh (new fees, results, admissions window) | ❌ | ✅ |
| New SEO topic pages (§3) | ❌ | 2 new pages per term |
| Google Business Profile + review nudges | ❌ | ✅ |
| **Billing (MoMo / bank)** | **100 000 FCFA/year** (2 months free) or 30 000/quarter | **250 000/year** or 70 000/quarter |

Math: one Basic Care client ≈ the founding build fee, **every year, with ~2 h/month
of work**. Four Care clients ≈ 400 k+ FCFA/year recurring baseline before any new
build — the local version of Erhart's "$297 × 10 clients". Hosting cost is near
zero (Vercel free tier + one shared domain); the fee pays for attention, speed and
the report.

Guardrails (standing rules respected):
- The founding **100 000 build price and 50/50 never moves and is never discounted**; Care is an OPTIONAL separate line offered at close, never a condition.
- Domain is registered **in the client's name** at close (already standard); Care is a service agreement, not host-age hostage. If they leave, files + domain transfer.
- Demo routing stays AMK until final payment (already rule; all videos agree: don't provision before payment).
- First two founding clients: propose Care verbally at their 30-day check-in, bundled with their testimonial ask — do not clutter the founder close itself.

## 5 · Monthly Care Report — fixed 1-page template (what justifies renewal)

Sent as a WhatsApp PDF/image, same day each month:
1. **Uptime**: days live, any downtime + what was done (free UptimeRobot-style monitor on the domain).
2. **Traffic**: visitors this month, top pages, phones vs computers, EN vs FR (Vercel Analytics or equivalent).
3. **Action metric**: WA button taps / booking refs (`SCH-`, `BMC-`, `MIT-` already generated) — the client's outcome number; for schools also "admission/fees pages most viewed".
4. **Changes made this month** (list, even if one line).
5. **Recommended next step** (one: add results page, add a price, request 3 Google reviews…).
Asset to build when first client goes live: `sales/templates/monthly-care-report.md` skeleton + a styled single-page HTML matching the client brand (built once, reused).

## 6 · Delivery upgrades adopted now (free, no client-facing change yet)

1. **Named-preview rule sharpened (Darrel):** within 24 h of a warm yes the concept carries THEIR crest/logo, real colours and quarter — never send a named prospect a second generic asset. Our builders + `tools/shots/` make this cheap; logo extraction from their FB page/photo is part of the 24 h.
2. **Concept sprint checklist (Ottley), internal:** 3–7 references (own token sheet first — never repeat a palette, `STYLE-TOKENS.md`) → one composite brief → generate → max 2 refinement passes → mobile + 3G pass → EN/FR pair audit → screenshot QA via `tools/shots/` → out. Target 60–90 min; obsessing pre-yes is the #1 margin killer Erhart warns about.
3. **Screenshot revision promise (Ferdy), say it at walkthrough/close:** "After launch, send me a screenshot with an arrow and a sentence — small changes come back the same week. That is included." Turns the maintenance fear into a reason to sign and pre-sells Care.
4. **60–90 s handoff video at every launch:** phone screen-recording of the live site — open it, tap EN→FR, tap the WhatsApp button, show their fees/results. Sent in the launch WhatsApp; it is training, proof, and a forwardable referral asset in one.
5. **AI fact-check gate (Erhart's warning #2 = our RESEARCH-STANDARD):** every generated page gets the standard pre-send audit (phone numbers, names, prices, claims) before a prospect sees it — non-negotiable, already law.
6. **Pre-payment provisioning gate (Erhart warning #3):** preview link stays AMK-hosted; domain/MoMo/real WA number switch only after the second 50 k. Already rule; restated.

## 8 · Google-Maps no-website sweep (prospecting SOP, 45 minutes/week)

Darrel + Erhart both source from Google Maps; in Cameroon this complements our
GCE/directory/press research and finds clinics especially.
1. Maps search: "[niche] in [city/quarter]" — e.g. "clinic Molyko Buea", "laboratory Douala Bonabéri", "optical shop Buea", "secondary school Bonduma".
2. For each listing record into the CRM: name, quarter, phone/WA (does the WhatsApp icon appear?), **website present yes/no — no = lead**, Google rating/review count (reviews + no website = strongest signal, à la Solidarity), Facebook link, gatekeeper/contact name if shown.
3. Cross-check governance: lay/private = OWNER-BUYER active; missionary/diocesan/government = BOARD park (standing rule).
4. No-site owners go into the normal flow: mockup msg 1 → consent → named concept.
Erhart's US data: ~13% of listed businesses had no site; expect a multiple of that here — note our own rate as we sweep.
