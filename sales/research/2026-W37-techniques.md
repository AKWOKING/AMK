## Technique 1 — Personalized async video in the sales sequence (30–60s)

**What it is:** a short screen-recording or face+screen video sent at the moments that matter: (a) as the "demo" after a permission ask, (b) after a walkthrough as a recap, (c) as a follow-up to a stalled conversation. It works because it carries effort and a human face where text reads as homework.

**Evidence**
- Sales outreach with personalized video produces roughly 2–3x the reply rate of text alone (Vidyard benchmark reporting; aggregated across the 2025–26 guides) [1](https://test.uniindia.com/news/press-releases/story/3859062.html) [2](https://blog.messagear.com/how-to-sell-guide/).
- Videos under 60 seconds get ~42% more engagement than longer ones; the highest CTRs come from clips under 90 seconds [5](https://www.autobound.ai/blog/top-10-video-outreach-platforms-for-sales-2026).
- Post-demo video recaps lift demos-booked/next-step rates (+46% reported for post-demo video follow-through) [5](https://www.autobound.ai/blog/top-10-video-outreach-platforms-for-sales-2026).
- HubSpot data cited in practitioner guides: personalized sales video ~+80% replies vs text email [3](https://www.mysalescoach.com/blog/video-prospecting).
- Thumbnails showing the prospect's own name/site lift click-through; generic video barely beats text [4](https://www.sendspark.com/resources/video-prospecting-guide).

**Fold into the playbook (proposed additions after this week's test):**
1. **Demo delivery becomes: file + 30-sec video, never file alone.** The video is the "future-pace on tape": open by naming the verified fact, show the single section that answers it (OraCare = price cards + booking; Sasse = results + admissions), end with the Cardone question.
2. **Shot-length discipline mirrors the 5-line rule:** one point per 10 seconds, ≤ 60s for first demos, ≤ 90s for walkthrough recaps.
3. **Thumbnail rule for future batches:** start the recording on a screen bearing the lead's own name (the concept header does this already) — the first frame is the thumbnail.
4. **Post-walkthrough recap becomes standard:** after every call, send a 45–90s recap video + the three agreed next steps before the end of the day (over-delivery tactic C8, now visual).

**This week's experiment (already committed):** King's OraCare message offered a "30-second video demo." Storyboard is in `Outreach-Pack-2026-09-14.md` §1. Record on reply, send same day. Success measure: video-branch reply rate vs file-branch; note it in the tracker.

## Technique 2 — Google Business Profile as the #1 local-search asset (and a new wedge for clinics/schools)

**What it is:** For local-intent searches ("dentist in Buea", "école bilingue Douala"), the map pack dominates mobile results. The single largest controllable lever is a complete, active Google Business Profile: category + full service list, photos on a cadence, reviews, hours, booking link. This is adjacent to the website and naturally bundles with it.

**Evidence**
- GBP signals are estimated at ~32% of local-pack ranking weight — the largest single factor in the BrightLocal factors work as applied to dental [1](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/dental-local-seo-statistics).
- Dental-seo practice audits name GBP management, suburb-level pages, and review generation as the three must-haves; the fastest free win is listing every treatment individually in the service list (~20 minutes) [2](https://aionmarketing.co.za/seo-services-for-dentists-in-south-africa/).
- A practice case (community-reported, treat as anecdote): service-list + geotagged weekly photos + review cadence → +42% map actions (calls/directions) and 15+ tracked bookings in 45 days with zero ad spend [4](https://www.reddit.com/r/localseo/comments/1u4yx38/case_study_how_a_simple_google_business_profile/).
- ~97% of consumers search online for local businesses and ~46% of Google searches carry local intent; reviews + photos + an appointment link drive the conversion [5](https://insidea.com/blog/marketing/dentists/local-seo-guide/).
- GBP changes move visibility on a 60–90 day horizon [2](https://aionmarketing.co.za/seo-services-for-dentists-in-south-africa/) — so starting during admissions/ad season is a timing pitch.

**Fold into the playbook (proposed):**
1. **Deliverable bundle upgrade (no price change for founding clients):** every launch includes (a) GBP claimed/verified in the client's name, (b) full bilingual service/programme list, (c) hours + the new site link + WhatsApp, (d) a 12-photo upload list, (e) a one-line review request template. Add these as checkboxes to `AMK-Internal-Delivery-Checklist.docx` at next edit.
2. **New clinic wedge line (verified-fact dependent):** "When patients search 'dentist Buea', there is no listing to find and the ad lands on a Facebook page. The site plus a complete Google listing is what makes the ad pay." OraCare has **no GBP listing at all** — verified in the Deep-Dive; this is a legitimate, specific pain.
3. **Paid upsell after launch:** monthly GBP + reviews + photo cadence ("Google visibility plan"), one of the recurring-revenue products needed for the 500k/month target.
4. **AMK's own GBP (already submitted) becomes proof:** once verified, screenshot its impressions/actions monthly and use the numbers in pitches.

**This week's action:** AMK GBP verification answer (King); add the GBP checklist to the OraCare/Sasse kickoff talking points; no client claims until our own profile is live.

## Technique 3 — Chat-first commerce on WhatsApp is the SSA default (defend the channel + price the assistant)

**What it is:** In Cameroon's market, WhatsApp is not a convenience channel, it is the default commercial surface. This (a) validates WA-first as architecture rather than preference, (b) reframes the scripted 24/7 assistant as a product with a value story, (c) gives sober benchmarks for what "messaging a business" means to buyers here.

**Evidence**
- 93% of African knowledge workers prefer WhatsApp for work-related communication over email (78%), Zoom (48%) or Teams (27%) — KnowBe4 Africa 2025 report [3](https://technext24.com/2025/02/18/african-users-prefer-whatsapp-email-zoom/).
- A Meta-commissioned SSA study: over 80% of adults message a business at least weekly; 75% feel more connected to brands they can message, 68% are more likely to buy from one reachable on WhatsApp [4](https://www.mynewsgh.com/how-whatsapp-business-api-is-reshaping-customer-communication-in-africa-key-trends-and-statistics/).
- Businesses using WhatsApp as their primary sales channel report ~2.4x higher monthly revenue growth than businesses not using digital channels (SSA reporting round-up) [2](https://www.amraandelma.com/whatsapp-marketing-statistics/).
- WhatsApp business messaging showed double-digit growth across Africa in 2025; businesses using it report 120–127% improvements in conversation/conversion metrics in Infobip's aggregate reporting [1](https://www.infobip.com/blog/whatsapp-statistics).
- Adoption among internet users in leading African markets is reported at 95–97% [5](https://techrt.com/whatsapp-business-usage-statistics/).

**Fold into the playbook (proposed):**
1. **One-liner for objection #3 ("we already have a Facebook page"):** add, "In Cameroon, parents expect to *message* a school like they message family; a website with a WhatsApp button is a front office that never closes, a feed is not." Keep it conversational, never a stat dump.
2. **The 24/7 assistant becomes a priced add-on post-launch:** ship the scripted version in the concept (as OraCare v3 does); after handover, quote a "reception assistant" upgrade fed with the client's real answers, and later a real AI version (already flagged in v1's footnote). This is the first named upsell beyond e-learning and GBP care.
3. **Form rule stays strict:** forms open WhatsApp pre-filled (we already do this); nothing sends silently, the client always sees the message before it leaves — important for trust with first-time digital buyers.
4. **Prospecting channel implication:** for FR Douala leads without WhatsApp numbers, Messenger is the correct primary (COMOBIL plan already does this); for clinics, WA verified number is an MQL gate item.

**This week's action:** none new to send; the OraCare v3 handover already demonstrates the assistant, and the close conversation can name it as "included in your concept, upgradeable later" without pricing it until asked.
