## 1 · The five headline numbers (memorise these)

1. **~9–10 million Cameroonians are on WhatsApp** (80% of 12.4M internet users,
   42% population penetration, Jan 2025). **88% of connected Cameroonians have
   bought something through WhatsApp**; 68% use Facebook pages/Marketplace
   commercially; 98% of social access is mobile-only. [askyazi WhatsApp Africa
   2026](https://www.askyazi.com/articles/whatsapp-penetration-across-africa-statistics-by-country) ·
   [Hilltop Voices, Cameroon social stats 2026](https://www.hilltopvoices.com/2026/07/cameroons-social-media-boom-reshapes.html)
   → *Line:* **"Your next patient or parent already lives in WhatsApp — 9 million of us do."**
2. **11.4 million active Mobile Money accounts (2024), 89% of all digital
   transactions pass through MoMo/Orange, merchant payments grew +85% since
   2023.** Cameroon transacts on the phone. [Cameroun24 / telecom observatory
   2024](https://cameroun24.net/article/71870-mobile-money-data-et-nouveaux-rapports-de-force-2024-lannee-du-grand-basculement-dans-les-telecoms-au-cameroun.html) ·
   [Simiz payment guide 2026](https://simiz.io/blog/guide-paiement-mobile-cameroun-2026)
3. **MINESEC has legally digitised ALL secondary schools, public AND private:**
   unique matricule per pupil, school card, fee payment only via
   cartescolaire.cm through MTN MoMo/Orange/Campost/Express Union/Afriland
   (in force since 2024–25; MTN MoMo partnership renewed June 2026). Minister
   Nalova Lyonga: **"La digitalisation est une réalité incontournable, et c'est
   le MINESEC qui doit en être le fer de lance."** [upactu, Aug 2024](https://upactu.com/matricule-unique-nouvelle-etape-de-la-digitalisation-de-lenseignement-secondaire-au-cameroun/) ·
   [cameroun-online, Sep 2026](https://www.cameroun-online.com/blog/frais-scolaires-paiement-en-ligne-cartescolaire-matricule-unique-rentree-2026-2027) ·
   [Télé'Asu](https://teleasu.tv/carte-scolaire-les-parents-a-lepreuve-de-cette-reforme-numerique-du-minesec/) ·
   [MINESEC: 350 digital whiteboards to principals, Apr 2025](https://minesec.gov.cm/web/index.php/fr/?option=com_content&view=article&id=655&catid=7&lang=fr-FR)
   → *Line:* **"The Ministry already put your school inside a digital system.
   Your own website is the front door parents see first — being invisible while
   paying fees went digital is the wrong look."**
4. **Health is a declared national digital frontier:** National Digital Health
   Strategic Plan 2020–2024, **11.32 billion FCFA**; May 2025 Minsanté–CAMTEL
   five-year accord connecting health facilities. **Yet only 32.1% of facilities
   own a computer and 27% have internet (peer-reviewed 2025).** [AITN, May 2025](https://afriqueitnews.com/tech-media/ameroun-connecte-hopitaux-sante-2-0/) ·
   [digital-health landscape study, 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12605872/)
   → *Line:* **"Government is spending 11 billion to drag health online; the
   private clinics patients choose every day can't be the ones still invisible."**
5. **The local price ceiling is public and high:** Krexora (Akwa, Douala) from
   **450 000 FCFA** + maintenance **from 80 000 FCFA/month**; HuginMboa Douala
   from ~200 000; Webmaster-Freelance Yaoundé from 150 000. AMK founding 100 000
   is the cheapest *credible* bilingual offer in the market. [Krexora](https://krexora.com/) ·
   [HuginMboa](https://www.huginmboa.com/) · [Webmaster Freelance CM](https://www.webmaster-freelance.cm/)
   → *Line:* **"Douala agencies bill 150 to 450 thousand for a French-only
   vitrine. The 100 000 is the two-slot founding price — your slot locks it."**

## 3 · The trust / fraud wave — why an "official-looking" presence converts

- Before the Matricule Unique, **fraudsters posed as school-fee operators and
  extorted millions from parents** (ICLAN consultant/MINESEC, Aug 2024,
  upactu). Double payments and APEE rackets are long-running press scandals.
  [koaci 2021](https://www.koaci.com/article/2021/08/31/cameroun/societe/cameroun-rentree-scolaire-pris-entre-arnaques-et-corruption-les-chefs-detablissements-publics-rappeles-a-lordre_153537.html)
  → *Line:* **"Parents have been scammed by fake fee collectors and fake result
  sites. Your official website — your crest, your number, your fees — is how a
  parent proves the school they are paying is the real one."**
- OBC 2025: **255 people sanctioned for exam fraud (208 candidates, 47
  teachers), WhatsApp leak networks named**; scam sites charge panicked
  students for "instant GCE results" while the Board never charges.
  [Stop Bla Bla Cam, Jun 2026](https://www.stopblablacam.com/societe/0906-16293-malgre-les-sanctions-et-les-reformes-la-fraude-continue-de-fragiliser-les-examens-officiels-au-cameroun) ·
  [checkresults.co guide](https://checkresults.co/exams/how-to-check-cameroon-gce-results-2026-complete-guide-for-o-level-a-level-candidates/)
- **Results day = national internet traffic spike.** Thousands in Cameroon AND
  the diaspora flood camgceb.org at release; third-party sites (kamerpower,
  apps) capture the spillover. **A school that hosts its own honour roll /
  pass-rate PDF / prize-giving photos on results day gets shared by every proud
  parent and old student** — this is the student-facing reason to visit the
  site that King asked for, now built into the school sample as
  **Student Corner / Espace Élèves**.
- Counterfeit economy estimated at 100 bn FCFA/year losses (Minesec/MINT
  cited) — a culture where visible officiality itself sells. [camer.be](https://www.camer.be/93930/30:27/point-de-vue)

## 5 · Channel facts for the inbound pivot (TikTok / IG, King's choice 15 Sep)

- **TikTok: 4M+ Cameroonian users**, ages skewing 13–30; **73% of consumers
  prefer short-form video to discover products/services; short video earns
  2.5× the engagement.** TikTok Shop launched Cameroon late 2024.
  [Biro Integrated, Oct 2025](https://birointegratedtech.com/digital-marketing-trends-cameroon-2026/)
- **Instagram: ~2.2M users, concentrated Douala/Yaoundé/Bafoussam, 18–35** —
  right audience for clinic owners/young proprietors. WhatsApp still closes
  (88%); FB still widest (85% of social users) but King chooses TikTok/IG for
  the gift push; TikTok videos can mirror to FB Page with zero extra work.
  [Biro: social platforms CM 2026](https://birointegratedtech.com/social-media-marketing-for-cameroon-businesses/) ·
  [Biro: WA leads CM](https://birointegratedtech.com/whatsapp-business-cameroon/)
- SMEs: INS census found only **16.9% of enterprises had internet** (2018);
  the 650-owner Econuma TPE/PME study still shows a large digital laggard
  majority (≈36% no office computer) — i.e. the no-website pool is large, but
  we must pick sectors where the BUYER feels the gap daily (clinics, boarding
  schools). [RAG 2020 study](https://www.rag.sn/document/61) · [Econuma study](https://econuma.com/go-digital/etude-sur-la-transformation-digitale-de-la-tpepme-au-cameroun-1616772824)

## 7 · What NOT to claim (accuracy guardrails)

- Never say a website is required by MINESEC or Minsanté — it is not; the
  mandate covers fee systems/connectivity, not marketing sites. Say the
  *sector* is being digitised and the front door should match.
- Never quote a stat we cannot produce a source for if asked; keep this
  dossier link. Industry blogs (Biro, Simiz) are "industry estimates", not
  government truth; prefer ANTIC/We Are Social/observatoire figures in formal
  writing.
- Do not promise Google rankings ("page 1 in a week"), MoMo fee collection
  for regulated school fees, or GCE result data feeds.
- camgceb.org is the only official Board results URL; our Student Corner links
  there, never to paid third-party result sites.
