# AMK Prospect Research Standard (accuracy law for outreach intelligence)

_Created 15 Sep after the Solidarity dossier wrongly named a doctor who died in 2020 (Dr Peter Nde Fon, CEO/founder) by trusting a stale aggregator roster and a truncated PDF name. Rule: no fact, name, or number reaches a message without clearing this standard. A polished message built on wrong facts is worse than no message: it burns the lead and AMK's credibility._

## 1. The failure this standard prevents (error log entry #1)
1. WTWC 2019 PDF said "SOLIDARITY CLINIC DR NDIFOR". A newspaper quote mentioning "Dr Ndifor of Solidarity" was treated as confirmation. Both were the same truncated rendering of **Nde Fon**; the actual person, **Dr Peter Nde Fon**, was the founder/CEO and **died 21 Oct 2020**. A different man, **Dr Peter Louis Ndifor** (Bota Polyclinic), was then conflated with him.
2. medicoor's doctor dropdown was treated as a current staff roster. It still listed the deceased founder in 2026 because the profile is unclaimed and never updated.
3. The second listed doctor (Dr Njang Mbeng Emmanuel) was assumed to currently work there; his own LinkedIn shows him as DMO Muyuka + co-owner of a different lab since 2023, with no Solidarity role listed.
4. Lesson: **aggregator pages are lead sources, not evidence. Nothing on them is in present tense until corroborated.**

## 2. Source tiers (every fact gets tagged)

| Tier | What it is | Use in outreach |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | The organisation's OWN current channels: owner-verified Google Business Profile, official Facebook/X/TikTok with posts in the last 6 months, official website, official NGO/government registry entry with the org as subject, the person's own LinkedIn with current dated role | Facts usable directly; still date them |
| **B** | Named person's professional profile, press interviews, peer-reviewed papers, professional-body registers | Usable after freshness + collision checks (§4) |
| **C** | Third-party directories/aggregators (medicoor, elidge, near-place, wanderboat, businesslist, mawidoO, yellow-page clones), AI summary sites | **Lead generation only.** Every fact must be corroborated by Tier A/B or King's phone verification before use |
| **D** | Dated documents (provider PDFs, old journals, 2010s blogspots), screenshots, YouTube descriptions | Use only with the date stated; names/numbers treated as historical, never current |
| **U** | User-generated content (reviews, comments, ratings) | Directional; rating numbers verified live on the platform at time of research; review text quoted only as themes, never as fact about staff |

Golden rule of tension: **when a Tier C source contradicts a Tier A/B source, Tier A/B wins every time.** When two C sources agree, that is still only Tier C confidence, not confirmation.

## 3. Every dossier fact is dated and labeled
Each row in a dossier/CRM carries one of:
- `[VERIFIED]` = Tier A/B source, fetched during research, source URL + date recorded
- `[LIKELY]` = 2+ independent Tier C sources agreeing (state how many)
- `[UNVERIFIED]` = single source or unresolved
- `[STALE]` = source predates a known change (death, move, closure) or is older than ~24 months with no corroboration
- `[KING-VERIFY]` = only his phone/WhatsApp check can settle it (number ownership, WA profile name)

No message 1 may contain: a personal name, a rating, a "you have no X" claim, or an employment/role fact that is below `[VERIFIED]` for that exact claim. When unsure, the message asks the recipient to route it (the gatekeeper pattern), which also extracts the correct attribution.

## 4. Named-person checklist (MANDATORY before any person's name enters outreach)
1. **Full-name search**: full name in quotes + org + city. Capture every distinct person the search returns.
2. **Collision check**: list all name-alikes found (e.g., Nde Fon vs Ndifor vs Njang) and explicitly state why the chosen person is the right one. Two independent sources must agree on BOTH the full name AND the organisation.
3. **Status/obituary search (non-negotiable)**: `"<full name>" obituary OR tribute OR "is no more" OR death OR buried OR late`. If the person is deceased, STOP: they become historical context; find who succeeded them (family statement, board, current staff).
4. **Current-role dating**: open their LinkedIn/professional profile; the current role at the target org must carry a date range that reaches the present, or be corroborated by a source dated within the last 12 months. "Listed on a directory" is not employment.
5. **Conflict check**: does the person own or work for a competing business? A clinic doctor running their own lab changes both the routing and the pitch; flag before contact.
6. **Authority/governance check (who actually signs?):** for every school/clinic dossier classify the buyer:
   - **OWNER-BUYER** (lay private, proprietor decides) → normal cycle, principal/proprietor is the target.
   - **BOARD/CHURCH-BUYER** (missionary/confessional: PCC/Presbyterian, Baptist Mission, Catholic diocesan/religious-order; government colleges) → the principal CANNOT sign; decisions sit with an education secretary, senior clerk, moderator, diocesan education office or board, often with network-wide policy. Evidence of a *deliberate network-wide pattern* (e.g. no PCC secondary school anywhere has a website) means absence is policy, not neglect, and a site-level pitch is a dead end. Route: inbound only, or one network-level proposal to the headquarters authority (long cycle; never on the 30-day close plan).
   - Label every lead `OWNER-BUYER` / `BOARD-BUYER` / `UNKNOWN-governance` before msg 1; BOARD-BUYER leads get no 3-FU chase and no counted slot.
7. **Precedent claim check:** "others in their network already do X" needs TWO verified network members actually doing X, same denomination/network (a congregation-founded IDP spin-off is not a denominational precedent). King's ground-truth on local institutions overrides web inference; when he corrects a structural claim, reclassify the whole class, not just the lead.
8. Output: one line per person — `Name | role at target | evidence (url, date) | status: confirmed current / historical / unverified / deceased`.

## 5. Phone/WhatsApp number attribution (confidence labels)
- **ORG-CONFIRMED**: on the org's own channel (Google profile, official FB) AND King has seen the WhatsApp profile.
- **ORG-LIKELY**: appears on 2+ independent sources tied to the organisation (not scraped mirrors of one another; check).
- **PERSONAL-NAMED**: attributed to a specific person anywhere → it is that person's line, NOT the organisation's. Never use it as the clinic's front door unless that person's current role there clears §4. If King says "this is his direct WhatsApp", that settles ownership as personal.
- **UNKNOWN**: one listing only.
Record every number with its label, its source, and what it likely is (reception / booking / doctor / personal / landline). Numbers die and move; a 2019 PDF number is `[STALE]` until a live source repeats it.

## 6. Claim audit for message 1
Before King sends, list every factual claim the message makes, each mapped to a source:
| Claim in message | Source | Tier | Date | Pass? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "54 patient reviews and 21 photos" | Google Business Profile fetched 15 Sep | A | 15 Sep | yes |
| "no website attached" | GBP "Add website" button seen | A | 15 Sep | yes |
| Named doctor | (would have been medicoor roster) | C | undated | NO |
A claim the recipient could factually dispute, or that addresses a dead/moved/wrong person, fails the send. Re-verify live platform numbers (ratings change; King's "4.5" was live at 4.0).

## 7. Sourcing order (the actual search sequence)
1. Google the exact business name + city; open the **Google Business Profile** first: reviews count, rating, photos, hours, website button (present or "Add website"), phone, address, plus code.
2. Official social: Facebook page (last post date), X, TikTok, Instagram; note owner/admin names visible.
3. Official registries relevant to the sector: UN NGO Branch (esango.un.org) for foundations/associations, insurance provider lists (date the PDF), medical council/ministry lists, chamber of commerce.
4. The named people: LinkedIn (role dates), press, obituary search (§4.3), ResearchGate/Google Scholar for academics.
5. Aggregators last (medicoor, elidge, near-place, etc.): harvest candidate numbers/names, then attribute each via steps 1-4.
6. Numbers: search the full number in quotes (multiple formats: `6XX XX XX XX`, `6XXXXXXXX`, `+237...`) to see who it is attached to across sources.
7. Exclusion sweep: own website (park the lead), maintained socials + store (park), active booking platform they own (park).
8. Sanity search on the business name + `"closed" OR "relocated" OR "no more" OR fire`.

## 8. Dossier definition of done (before a message is drafted)
- [ ] Org legal name, operating name, ownership type (individual / family / foundation/CIG / company) with source
- [ ] Current decision-maker: confirmed current person OR explicitly "unknown; message routes via gatekeeper" with a fallback
- [ ] Every named person cleared §4 (collision + obituary + current-role + conflict), results written down
- [ ] Every number labeled per §5; at least one ORG-CONFIRMED/ORG-LIKELY front door or a physical-card route
- [ ] Pain claims from Tier A/B evidence only (or clearly marked as inference from absence, e.g., no website button on GBP)
- [ ] Claim audit table (§6) filled for the drafted message
- [ ] Date of research on the dossier; facts older than 30 days re-verified before sending if send date slips
- [ ] Any negative result stated plainly ("current director not found in public sources") instead of guessed

## 8b. PRE-SEND GATES — the three doors (standing, King approved 17 Sep 2026)

**Every send passes through this table, every time, no exceptions.** A dossier can be perfect and still fail here. Record the verdict in the dossier before King taps send.

| # | Gate | Pass condition (evidence, not vibes) | If it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Reachability** | At least one number **verified live on WhatsApp by King** (profile seen), ideally **ORG-CONFIRMED** (§5). The channel we actually use must exist. | **No send.** Park with the reason; reopen only if a verified line appears. |
| **B · Digital intent** | A **recent** sign the organisation acts on digital: FB post < 12 months, live site, active WA catalogue/business account, recent Google activity. | **Park as dormant** (write the re-open condition). Dormant orgs default to "no decision", which costs the 30-Sep window. |
| **C · Buyer type** | Owner / decision-maker reachable (**OWNER-BUYER** label, §4.6). | Board buyer → cold leg only if the 30-day plan allows; otherwise park (no chase). |

**Scoring:** 3/3 = send. **2/3 = King decides explicitly**, and the reason is written down. 1/3 = never send.

**Worked example (JOSS MEDI, 17 Sep):** A **FAIL** (their own FB number 674 84 39 02 not on WhatsApp; no verified line) · B **FAIL** (last FB post 24 Jun 2022) · C **FAIL** (11–50 staff clinic since 2009 = board buyer) → **0/3, parked dormant.** Same day, same check on L'Opticien Bali: A **PASS** (WhatsApp Business account seen, catalogue in use) · B **PASS** (business account + catalogue + seen active 11:54) · C **PASS** (single-store optician = owner) → **3/3, send.**

## 9. Feedback loop
- Every King correction is logged here as an error-log entry with: what was wrong, which poisoned source caused it, what check would have caught it, and which OTHER dossiers used that source (they get re-checked the same day).
- When a source type proves unreliable in one dossier (here: medicoor doctor rosters + truncated PDF provider lists), every other dossier's facts from that source type are demoted one tier until re-verified.
