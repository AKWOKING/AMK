# Video 02 — 5 things your website should tell a customer in 10 seconds  (series Part 1)
Status: POSTED — IG 47 · TikTok 87 (King-reported; windows not supplied) · recorded 17 Sep 2026
Audience: clinic/school owners · Source: MboaCare (fictional BrightCare demo in the original) · Funnel: MOFU · Expected outcome: saves + profile visits

> Someone visits your website. You have about 10 seconds to answer five questions.
> Number one: What do you actually do?
> Number two: Who do you help?
> Number three: Why should I trust you?
> Number four: Where can I find you?
> And number five: How do I contact you?
> If your website makes people hunt for these answers, you're probably losing potential customers before they ever contact you.
> Save this and check your website.

**Visuals:** services, customer types, trust cues, location, contact — one question per screen.
**Payoff frame:** HARD TO FIND → EASY TO LEAVE.
⚠ "10 seconds" is a hook, NOT a measured abandonment threshold — never present it as a statistic.
