## Weekly cadence
- **Monday:** review pipeline, update calendar, plan the week against the sales outreach pack and the funnel's biggest need.
- **Wednesday:** mid-week check — renders in progress, drafts awaiting review, calendar adjustments.
- **Friday:** `pipeline/Weekly-Content-Report-YYYY-MM-DD.md` — produced, posted, funnel numbers per platform, learned, testing next week, rejected and why.
