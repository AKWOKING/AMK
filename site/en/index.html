What we will not do

Four things we will not sell you.

Every practice, shop and school site in Cameroon is sold with the same three promises. Here is what we refuse to add to the pile.

01

A price without its list

Our three prices are public: 150 000, 250 000, 450 000 FCFA. Above a figure with no list underneath, a price is not information, it is bait. So every amount here comes with what it includes and what makes it climb — and nothing is added to the bill after you have signed.

02

A deadline without its condition

“Live in 24 hours” means nothing without “once the brief is approved”. Every time we give a date, its condition sits in the same sentence, never in a footnote.

03

Reviews nobody wrote

An invented testimonial survives exactly as long as one phone call. Our proof is a site you can open right now, in this page, and walk through yourself.

04

A site that stays with us

Domain, files and hosting are in your name before the site goes live. If one day you want to leave, leaving is possible, and that is the only honest way to sell a website.

Questions

Asked before, answered honestly

How much does a website cost in Cameroon? 
Three formats, one public price each. Presence 150 000 FCFA: bilingual up to 3 pages, Google profile created, domain and hosting for the first year, 30-minute training. Standard 250 000 FCFA: up to 6 pages, Google profile optimised, one month of support. Catalogue 450 000 FCFA and up: up to 30 references, orders by WhatsApp. Upkeep is separate: 30 000 or 75 000 a month, no contract. The preview is free, and nothing is owed until you approve.
Why are clinics and shops the focus, and not schools? 
Because school demand follows the calendar: strong before the new term, quiet once the year is running. A clinic, a practice or a shop needs to be found every month of the year. The school page stays, and we prepare new-term campaigns in advance — that is when they work.
How long does it really take?
We do not give a date before we know the scope. The preview reaches your phone within 24 hours of your first message; going live follows your approval.
My staff aren't technical. Can they update the site?
Yes, that's the point. We design for non-technical staff, run a 30-minute training, and stay reachable on WhatsApp. News, photos, events: your team handles it.
Is bilingual (EN|FR) really included in the price?
Yes, it's the standard on every site we build, not an add-on. A school or clinic in Cameroon serves both languages; your website should too.
Do you really work with clinics and practices, or is that an add-on?
Clinics, practices, opticians and shops are our base, all year. A real one is live on this page: DM OPTIQUE SARL, an optician in Bonabéri, on its own domain with its Google profile. Schools come back for the new term, with the same base.
Do I pay for the preview?
No. The preview is free, and nothing is charged until you approve it and a deposit begins the build.
What happens after launch?
if you want us to handle updates, two public plans take over: Care 30 000 FCFA a month (verified backups, updates, 2 Google posts, a one-page report, 2 h of edits) and Growth 75 000 FCFA a month (Care plus 8 social pieces, 4 Google posts and managed ads). No contract, and your domain and files stay 100% yours.
Do you work with schools and clinics outside Douala?
Yes, we work remotely, anywhere in Cameroon. Everything happens on WhatsApp: the preview, the revisions, the launch.
Can I see a real website before deciding?
Yes, and the first one is real: DM OPTIQUE SARL, an optician in Bonabéri, is live on dmoptiquesarl.com with its Google profile. Below it, six complete concepts open from your phone: three for clinics, three for schools. The concepts use fictional names — we never show a client's site without written permission.
Will my school or clinic be found on Google?
We build with local search in mind, fast pages, clean structure, both languages, and the way parents and patients actually search: “school in Buea”, “dentist in Buea”, “école privée à Douala”. We set you up to be found; your reputation does the rest.
